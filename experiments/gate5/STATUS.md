# Gate 5 — Structural self-organization

**Status: FROZEN.**

★ A directory here is not a launch. Frozen means no new runs, no new criteria, and no
reading of the legacy prototype's structural results as evidence that this gate has been
tested.

## Why it is frozen rather than planned

The legacy pipeline (`legacy/pipeline_v1/`) contains a structural-development mechanism —
energy budget, birth and death, pruning. That mechanism is **not** an instance of this
gate: it was built for a different question, under different criteria, and its records
stay in the legacy archive. Frozen preserves that separation.

## What reopening would require

A decision record that fixes the criterion for "self-organization" before any run, plus
the launching conditions. Until then this gate stays closed regardless of what the legacy
code can already compute.
